## Aggregation Rules

- `resolve_hero`:
  - When the result is `resolved`, creates `hero_identity` evidence.
- `stratz.hero_vs_hero_matchup`:
  - Creates `matchup_win_rate` evidence from `advantage` / `disadvantage`.
  - Creates `sample_size` evidence for every matchup row.
- Unknown tools:
  - Produce no evidence.
- Failed tools:
  - Add `<tool_call_id>: tool_failed` to `missing`.
- `required_evidence`:
  - Any required kind without evidence is added to `missing`.

## Data Quality

The current EvidenceGraph computes:

- `mock_used`
- `min_sample_size`
- `completeness`

`completeness` is based on `required_evidence` coverage.
