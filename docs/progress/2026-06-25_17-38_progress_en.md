  - Allowed tools in the system prompt now come from the registry.
  - `stratz.hero_vs_hero_matchup` and `stratz.lane_outcome` both reject hardcoded `hero_id`; they must reference `"$resolve_target.data.hero.hero_id"`.

## Behavior Boundaries

- OpenDota tools return structured evidence only; they do not generate a team report.
- Team/meta evidence plans can execute into an EvidenceGraph, but unsupported synthesis returns `unsupported_output_contract`.
- OpenDota/STRATZ failures are exposed through `ToolResult.status="error"` and do not fall back to mock data.
- `/api/v1/query`, `/debug/chat`, and the legacy report pipeline were not modified.
