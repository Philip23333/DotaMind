## Resolution Strategy

- Normalize first:
  - trim
  - lowercase
  - remove apostrophes
  - convert `-` / `_` to spaces
  - collapse repeated spaces
- Matching order:
  - exact `localized_name`
  - exact internal hero slug
  - exact aliases
  - fuzzy candidates
- Three-state result:
  - `resolved`: one unique match.
  - `ambiguous`: multiple reasonable candidates, for example `es` maps to both Earthshaker and Earth Spirit.
  - `not_found`: no candidate found.
